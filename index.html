Hi! I'm a senior undergraduate in Intelligent Robotics at Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SeoulTech), and a Research Intern at the MLCS Lab, Yonsei University. Before that, I was an undergraduate researcher at MoAI Lab, SeoulTech, and a research intern at the Field Robotics Laboratory, ETRI.

My research interests lie in Robot Manipulation, Imitation Learning, and Vision-Language-Action Models. I want to build robot policies that hold up in the real world — learning efficiently from limited data and staying reliable across the long sequences of steps that real tasks demand.
